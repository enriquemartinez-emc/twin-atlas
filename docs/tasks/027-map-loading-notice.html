A loading notice for the map

Task 027In progressfeat/map-loading-notice

Google's 3D tiles keep arriving for several seconds after the viewer is up, so the screen looked empty. A notice in the middle of the map now says what is going on until the first view is drawn.

How to see it

Reload the app. A card with a spinner appears in the middle of the map and goes away when the first view is loaded. I have not seen it in a browser yet.

What was wrong

The only indicator, a spinner in the status bar, stopped as soon as the 3D viewer had been created. Loading the photorealistic tiles starts after that, and nothing said so.

What was built

  • The map says when it is ready. MapViewer has a tilesReady signal. For Google's tiles it turns true when the tileset reports that the tiles of the initial view are loaded. For the OpenStreetMap globe it turns true when the globe reports all its tiles loaded. It goes back to false when the viewer is rebuilt, for example after a token is saved.
  • One notice, in the middle. It says "Starting the 3D viewer…" and then "Loading the map…", with a line about Google's tiles being slow when that is the source. Clicks pass through it, and screen readers hear it as a status.
  • It admits when it is slow. After 15 seconds it adds that the map needs an internet connection. That uses Angular's @defer timer, so there is no timer code to clean up.
  • One indicator, not two. The old spinner left the status bar, which now shows nothing until the source of the map is known.

How it was checked

CheckResult
Tests273 client tests pass (264 before). New: the three ways the first view becomes ready and the reset, the notice texts and its slow message, and the page showing it while loading, hiding it when ready and hiding it after a startup error.
Deliberate breaksSix, each failed a test: Google tiles never marking ready, the globe ready at once, destroy keeping the ready flag, the notice never hiding, the notice showing over a startup error, and the title never saying the viewer is starting.
BuildFormatting, lint with the class check and the production build are clean. The lazy map-viewer chunk is 8.1 kB.

Not verified

How long the notice stays up on a real Google tileset, and whether the signals fire at the right moment with the real Cesium. The tests use a stand-in for Cesium. Checking needs your approval to drive the running app.

Left out

  • A progress bar. Cesium reports how many requests are pending, not how many there will be in total.
  • A notice when you move the camera and more tiles stream in.
